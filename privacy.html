Privacy Policy for Knowledge Park

Last Updated: August 7, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Knowledge Park. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you as to how we look after your personal data when you use our mobile application and tell you about your privacy rights.

2. Data We Collect

We may collect, use, and store the following types of data:

  • Identity Data: Name, email address, and profile picture (if logging in via Google).
  • Usage Data: Information about how you use our app, test scores, and educational progress.
  • Device Data: Anonymous crash reports and performance data to help us improve the app.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data strictly for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the educational services within the app.
  • To manage your account and authenticate your login securely via Firebase.
  • To track your study progress and test scores.
  • To improve app stability and user experience.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is securely stored using Google Firebase infrastructure. We implement appropriate security measures to prevent your personal data from being accidentally lost, used, or accessed in an unauthorized way.

5. Sharing Your Data

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties. This does not include trusted third-party services (like Google Firebase) that assist us in operating our app, so long as those parties agree to keep this information confidential.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you and to ask that your personal data be corrected, updated, or deleted. If you wish to exercise this right, please contact us.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or our privacy practices, please contact the Knowledge Park development team.
